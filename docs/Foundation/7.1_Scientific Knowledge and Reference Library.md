### 7.1 — Scientific Knowledge and Reference Library

The application should provide a comprehensive, structured scientific knowledge library covering astronomy and related scientific disciplines in considerable depth. It should serve as a reliable reference resource for beginners, students, amateur astronomers, educators and experienced researchers.

The library should extend beyond introductory explanations, providing substantial scientific material covering foundational principles, advanced concepts, mathematical and physical theory, observational methods, scientific models and their applications.

**Scientific Coverage:**
- Astronomy and astrophysics fundamentals.
- Exoplanet science and planetary astronomy.
- Stellar astronomy and stellar physics.
- Spectroscopy and astrochemistry.
- Photometry and observational techniques.
- Atmospheric physics and radiative transfer.
- Planetary and stellar atmospheres.
- Orbital mechanics and astronomical systems.
- Mathematics, physics and chemistry relevant to the application's scientific capabilities.
- Scientific methodologies, models, equations, assumptions and limitations.
- Connections between different astronomical and scientific disciplines.

The library should expand alongside the application's scientific capabilities, ensuring that relevant subjects receive sufficient depth to support both general learning and practical scientific investigations.

**Presentation and Accessibility:**
- Organize information into clearly structured subjects, topics and subtopics.
- Present introductory explanations before progressing into more advanced material where appropriate.
- Allow users to explore scientific subjects progressively, without unnecessarily overwhelming them.
- Provide substantial detail without sacrificing readability or scientific accuracy.
- Make advanced mathematical, physical and theoretical material accessible without removing its complexity.
- Explain scientific terminology, principles, equations and relationships.
- Use diagrams, illustrations, graphs, equations and examples where they improve understanding.
- Allow users to explore related topics and follow connections between scientific disciplines.
- Avoid reducing advanced subjects to superficial summaries merely to make them accessible.

**External Scientific Sources and Further Reading:**

The library should connect users to external scientific resources so they can investigate subjects beyond the material presented within the application.

- Provide references to the original scientific sources used when researching and developing the application's knowledge library.
- Include additional relevant sources that expand upon, explain or provide alternative perspectives on the material being discussed.
- Prioritize authoritative scientific resources, including peer-reviewed publications, textbooks, university materials, official scientific documentation and established research databases.
- Provide direct links to available publications, papers, documentation, datasets and other relevant resources.
- Where practical, identify the specific sections, chapters or publications relevant to the topic rather than providing generic links.
- Include bibliographic information, authorship, publication details and access dates where appropriate.
- Distinguish primary research, review articles, educational material, reference databases and other source types.
- Make it clear which sources were used to construct the application's explanation and which are additional recommended reading.
- Identify access restrictions, unavailable publications or other limitations where known.
- Preserve references so users can trace scientific explanations back to their supporting material.

**Knowledge Organization and Relationships:**
- Organize the library so related subjects can be discovered and explored together.
- Link relevant concepts, equations, methods, scientific models and external references.
- Connect educational material to relevant scientific tools and workflows elsewhere in the application.
- Allow users to move between introductory explanations, advanced theory, practical applications and original research.
- Maintain consistency between the scientific knowledge library and the scientific methodologies actually implemented in the application.

**General Principle:** Establish a substantial and scientifically credible reference library that supports independent learning and advanced investigation. Users should be able to understand a subject at an appropriate level of detail, explore it further when desired and trace the information back to reliable scientific sources.
