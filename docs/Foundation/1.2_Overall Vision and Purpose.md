# 1.2 — Overall Vision and Purpose

## Purpose

Establish the overall vision of creating a comprehensive, scientifically credible astronomy research platform that brings together the tools, data, knowledge and workflows needed to investigate astronomical subjects.

The application should provide an integrated environment in which users can discover scientific resources, explore astronomical objects, obtain and examine observational data, conduct analyses, develop models, visualize results and manage their research.

Scientific investigation is the application's primary purpose. Education, project management, data discovery, collaboration and community functionality should support and strengthen that purpose as integrated parts of the wider environment.

The application should accommodate a broad range of scientific interests and experience levels while maintaining the depth, reliability and flexibility expected of serious scientific software.

## 1.2.1 Central Scientific Purpose

**Core requirements**

- Establish astronomy research as the application's central purpose.
- Provide an environment for investigating astronomical objects, systems, phenomena and scientific questions.
- Support the discovery, retrieval, examination, analysis, modelling and interpretation of astronomical data.
- Bring relevant scientific tools and resources together within a coherent application.
- Support investigations that use observational evidence, established scientific methods, computational approaches and theoretical models.
- Allow users to examine scientific results, understand their supporting evidence and develop further investigations.
- Accommodate both focused investigations and broader research questions involving multiple astronomical subjects.
- Preserve scientific credibility, transparency and reproducibility throughout the research process.

The application should help users move beyond simply viewing astronomical information. It should support meaningful scientific investigation, from identifying a question and locating relevant evidence to examining results and developing further understanding.

## 1.2.2 Comprehensive Astronomy Research Platform

The application should bring together a broad range of scientific capabilities rather than concentrating on a single analytical method or astronomical discipline.

**Core requirements**

- Support multiple areas of astronomy and related scientific investigation.
- Provide specialized scientific capabilities appropriate to different research subjects.
- Support observational data analysis, scientific computation, modelling and visualization.
- Provide access to relevant astronomical databases, archives, catalogues, literature and external scientific resources.
- Support the use of established scientific software and external services where appropriate.
- Allow scientific capabilities to operate independently when necessary while remaining integrated into the wider application.
- Accommodate different research approaches without forcing every investigation into an identical workflow.
- Preserve opportunities to expand scientific coverage as the application develops.

The platform should provide sufficient breadth to support different types of astronomical research while allowing individual scientific capabilities to maintain the depth and specialization their work requires.

## 1.2.3 Unified Research Environment

The application should function as both a collection of scientific tools and a unified environment for conducting and managing research.

**Core requirements**

- Provide a consistent environment for accessing relevant scientific capabilities and resources.
- Allow users to move between research activities without unnecessarily losing context or duplicating work.
- Connect relevant astronomical objects, observations, datasets, analyses, models, visualizations and research records.
- Support the organization and continuation of investigations over time.
- Allow users to combine compatible tools and methods within broader research workflows.
- Preserve relationships between scientific resources and the results derived from them.
- Make relevant information available across appropriate parts of the application.
- Avoid presenting scientific capabilities as unrelated features with no meaningful connection.

Integration should be guided by scientific relevance and practical usefulness. Not every tool needs to be directly connected to every other tool, but related functionality should work together where doing so improves research continuity and efficiency.

## 1.2.4 Supporting Scientific Activities

Although scientific research is the application's primary purpose, effective research requires more than analytical tools alone.

The application should integrate supporting activities that help users discover, understand, organize and communicate scientific work.

**Supporting areas include:**

- **Data discovery:** Finding astronomical observations, datasets, catalogues, archives and other relevant scientific resources.
- **Education and knowledge:** Accessing scientific explanations, reference materials, learning resources and contextual guidance.
- **Project management:** Organizing investigations, research materials, analytical results and ongoing work.
- **Collaboration:** Supporting appropriate forms of shared research and coordinated scientific activity.
- **Community:** Providing opportunities for scientific discussion, knowledge exchange and interaction with other users.
- **Research communication:** Helping users document, present and share scientific methods, findings and supporting evidence.

These areas should be treated as meaningful components of the research environment rather than unrelated additions.

Their detailed requirements and boundaries are established in the relevant sections of this Foundations documentation.

## 1.2.5 Professional Scientific Capability and Accessibility

The application should combine substantial scientific capability with an approachable and understandable user experience.

**Core requirements**

- Support scientifically meaningful investigations rather than limiting functionality to simplified demonstrations.
- Provide access to advanced analytical and computational capabilities where appropriate.
- Accommodate users with different levels of scientific, mathematical and programming experience.
- Make complex scientific operations understandable through appropriate explanations, guidance and documentation.
- Allow users to progress from introductory activities to more advanced investigations.
- Avoid restricting advanced functionality simply because the application is also intended to be accessible to beginners.
- Avoid making ordinary research activities unnecessarily difficult through excessive complexity.
- Clearly distinguish established scientific results, user assumptions, approximations and exploratory interpretations.

Accessibility should come from thoughtful presentation, guidance and workflow design rather than reducing scientific depth or concealing important methodological details.

## 1.2.6 Breadth, Depth and Scientific Adaptability

The application's vision should be broad enough to accommodate different astronomical disciplines while allowing individual research areas to develop specialized capabilities.

**Core requirements**

- Support a broad range of astronomical research subjects.
- Allow scientific areas to have specialized tools, datasets, methods and workflows.
- Accommodate investigations that cross disciplinary boundaries.
- Avoid designing the entire application around the assumptions of one research discipline.
- Preserve the ability to introduce additional scientific subjects and capabilities.
- Allow scientific functionality to develop as new research requirements become clear.
- Prioritize scientific usefulness and methodological suitability over superficial feature coverage.

The application should not attempt to make every scientific discipline identical. Its unified environment should provide consistency where useful while respecting the distinct requirements of different research areas.

## 1.2.7 Long-Term Scientific Value

The application should be developed as a long-term scientific platform capable of supporting increasingly sophisticated research.

**Core requirements**

- Establish a foundation that can accommodate additional scientific capabilities over time.
- Support the continued development and refinement of existing analytical methods.
- Allow new scientific datasets, resources and external integrations to be incorporated when appropriate.
- Preserve research records and results so investigations can be revisited and extended.
- Encourage reproducible and transparent scientific practices.
- Evaluate future capabilities according to their scientific value, reliability, feasibility and practical usefulness.
- Avoid making short-term implementation convenience the sole determinant of long-term application direction.

The initial application does not need to contain every capability described by the broader vision. Its development should proceed incrementally while preserving a coherent direction toward the intended platform.

## General Principles

- Astronomy research is the application's primary purpose.
- The application should provide a comprehensive environment for scientific investigation.
- Scientific tools, data, knowledge and research workflows should be integrated meaningfully.
- The platform should function as both a suite of scientific capabilities and a unified research environment.
- Education, project management, data discovery, collaboration and community functionality should support the central scientific purpose.
- Scientific depth and accessibility should coexist.
- Different research disciplines should retain the specialized capabilities they require.
- Scientific credibility, transparency, reproducibility and user control should guide the application.
- The platform should remain adaptable to new scientific requirements and future research directions.
- Development should be incremental, with priorities determined by scientific value and practical feasibility.

**General principle:** The application should provide a comprehensive and accessible environment for meaningful astronomy research, bringing together scientific capabilities, observational evidence, knowledge and research workflows while preserving the rigor, transparency and flexibility required for credible scientific investigation.

## Implementation Boundary

This subsection establishes the application's overall vision and purpose.

It does not define the complete scientific discipline list, individual scientific capabilities, detailed workflows, workspace arrangements, technical architecture or implementation priorities.

These matters are addressed in subsequent sections of the Foundations documentation and should be developed through the appropriate research and planning processes.
