# 7.0 — Education, Documentation and Learning Resources

## Section Purpose

This section establishes the educational, instructional and scientific reference resources provided by the application.

The educational system should support users in developing their understanding of astronomy and related scientific disciplines, ranging from foundational concepts to advanced scientific theory, mathematical methods and practical research techniques.

It should provide structured educational material, interactive learning activities, scientific explanations, mathematical references and access to relevant scientific literature. Users should be able to study independently, follow structured learning paths, explore scientific concepts through practical activities and develop their knowledge using relevant astronomical data and application capabilities.

The section also establishes requirements for user-created educational resources, community learning, scientific content quality and the validation of educational material.

Educational resources should be useful independently of the application's tools while also supporting users who wish to understand the scientific principles, methods and limitations underlying its functionality.

## Section Overview

Section 7 addresses nine interconnected areas:

1. **Scientific Knowledge and Reference Library:** Establishes a comprehensive, searchable scientific reference library covering astronomy and related disciplines, from foundational knowledge to advanced scientific concepts.
2. **Structured Learning Paths and Educational Courses:** Defines how users can develop their knowledge progressively through organized courses, learning paths, modules and lessons.
3. **Interactive Learning and Practical Exercises:** Establishes interactive demonstrations, simulations, practical activities and exercises that connect scientific concepts with observation, experimentation and analysis.
4. **Contextual Scientific Education:** Defines educational explanations and assistance that help users understand scientific concepts, methods, tools and terminology within relevant contexts.
5. **Mathematical and Scientific Explanations:** Establishes educational material covering mathematical foundations, physical and chemical principles, equations, derivations, calculations and their scientific applications.
6. **Scientific Literature and References:** Defines how users can discover, access, organize and evaluate scientific publications and bibliographic resources.
7. **User-Created Educational Resources:** Establishes how users can create, organize, maintain and reuse their own educational notes, tutorials, exercises and learning materials.
8. **Community Learning and Resource Sharing:** Defines how users can exchange scientific knowledge, discuss educational subjects and share learning resources and relevant research materials.
9. **Educational Content Quality and Validation:** Establishes requirements for scientific accuracy, source reliability, educational quality, review, correction and the responsible handling of community-created and AI-assisted content.

## General Principles

Educational and learning resources should:

- Support users with different levels of scientific knowledge and technical experience.
- Accommodate both independent study and structured learning.
- Provide sufficient depth to support advanced scientific understanding without unnecessarily overwhelming introductory learners.
- Connect scientific theory with practical applications, observations and research activities.
- Use accurate scientific explanations, appropriate mathematical notation and reliable references.
- Clearly distinguish established knowledge, theoretical models, approximations, hypotheses and scientific uncertainty.
- Support exploration across related scientific disciplines.
- Remain useful independently of the application's operational tools.
- Expand alongside the application's scientific capabilities and research areas.
- Distinguish official educational material, personal resources and community contributions.
- Maintain appropriate standards of scientific accuracy, source attribution and educational quality.

## Scope and Relationships with Other Sections

Section 7 establishes the educational content, learning resources and scientific reference environment provided by the application. It should remain coordinated with:

- **Section 3 — Core Scientific Capabilities and Research Workflows:** Provides the scientific functionality and methods that educational resources should explain and support. Educational material should reflect the application's actual scientific capabilities, including its scientific visualization and presentation requirements.
- **Section 4 — Data Discovery, Databases and External Resources:** Provides the data sources, scientific databases and external resources that may support educational activities and practical exercises.
- **Section 5 — Scientific Integrity, Automation and Analysis Control:** Establishes the scientific integrity, validation, uncertainty and reproducibility principles that educational material must respect.
- **Section 6 — Application Structure, Workspaces and User Experience:** Establishes how users access and interact with educational resources. In particular, Sections 6.6 and 6.9 address accessibility and contextual assistance within the application, while Section 7 establishes the underlying educational content and learning resources.
- **Section 8 — Community, Collaboration and Research Sharing:** Establishes the wider community and sharing environment supporting educational discussion, resource exchange and collaborative learning.

## Implementation Boundary

This section establishes the intended educational capabilities and content requirements without prescribing a fixed curriculum, complete course catalog, specific educational technology or implementation schedule.

The depth, organization and implementation of individual educational resources may evolve as the application's scientific capabilities, available resources and user needs develop.

Initial implementation priorities and later expansion should be determined through appropriate development planning, without treating every proposed educational capability as a requirement for the initial application release.

## General Principle

The educational system should function as a substantial scientific learning and reference environment in its own right, while remaining closely connected to the application's scientific capabilities, research workflows and wider community.

It should help users understand not only how to use the application, but also the scientific principles, mathematical foundations, methods and evidence underlying the research they conduct.
