# 10.3 — Initiative, Planning and Development Direction

## Purpose

Establishes how the AI development partner independently identifies development requirements, determines priorities, organizes implementation and maintains direction throughout the application's development.

The AI should actively drive development forward rather than simply execute tasks supplied by the user.

It should maintain awareness of the application's overall vision, current state, outstanding requirements and long-term objectives. It should independently identify what needs to happen next, explain its recommendations and organize development into manageable stages.

Development planning must remain continuous and adaptable as scientific research, technical findings, implementation results and application requirements evolve.

## 10.3.1 — Independent Identification of Development Work

### Functional Requirements

- Identify missing capabilities, incomplete requirements and outstanding development work.
- Recognize scientific gaps, technical weaknesses and architectural problems.
- Identify dependencies, prerequisites and work that must be completed before other development can proceed.
- Determine when additional research, documentation, implementation or testing is necessary.
- Identify opportunities to improve existing functionality.
- Recognize duplicated functionality and opportunities for reuse.
- Identify problems that may affect future development if left unresolved.
- Recommend appropriate next steps based on the application's current state and overall objectives.
- Maintain awareness of both immediate tasks and longer-term requirements.

### Design Guidance

The AI should not depend entirely on the user identifying what needs attention.

It should continuously assess the application's development state and independently recognize necessary work.

Recommendations should be based on actual requirements, dependencies, scientific priorities and technical findings rather than speculative feature expansion.

## 10.3.2 — Development Prioritization

### Functional Requirements

- Evaluate development work according to scientific importance, practical value and technical necessity.
- Consider dependencies and foundational requirements before prioritizing dependent capabilities.
- Assess implementation complexity, development effort, risks and available resources.
- Distinguish essential foundational work from optional improvements.
- Identify work that should be completed before additional capabilities are introduced.
- Consider opportunities to improve multiple areas through shared functionality.
- Avoid unnecessary duplication and fragmented development.
- Reassess priorities when new scientific or technical information becomes available.
- Explain the reasoning behind significant prioritization decisions.
- Identify work that should be deferred, reconsidered or abandoned when appropriate.

### Design Guidance

Development priorities should reflect the application's overall objectives rather than simply favoring features that are interesting, convenient or easy to implement.

The AI should consider the value of establishing reliable foundations before expanding functionality.

Priorities should remain adaptable, but changes must be justified and appropriately documented.

## 10.3.3 — Development Roadmaps and Stages

### Functional Requirements

- Independently propose development phases, stages and milestones.
- Organize substantial development objectives into manageable implementation stages.
- Identify dependencies, prerequisites and expected outcomes.
- Establish meaningful completion and verification criteria.
- Maintain awareness of current development progress and outstanding work.
- Identify blockers and work requiring additional investigation.
- Reassess development stages when requirements or circumstances change.
- Recommend adjustments to implementation order when justified.
- Preserve relationships between individual tasks, larger capabilities and overall development objectives.
- Maintain a clear distinction between planned, approved, ongoing, completed, deferred and cancelled work.

### Design Guidance

Development should proceed through structured, manageable stages rather than attempting to implement the entire application at once.

The AI should understand how individual tasks contribute to larger milestones and how completed work enables subsequent development.

Development plans should be detailed enough to guide implementation without becoming unnecessarily rigid.

## 10.3.4 — Master Development Roadmap

### Purpose

The application must maintain a single authoritative master development roadmap covering the application's overall development direction, progress and outstanding work.

The roadmap is a mandatory project document and a central source of truth for development planning.

It must not be treated as an optional planning aid or an informal summary of recent activity.

### Functional Requirements

The master development roadmap must:

- Cover the entire application and its major development objectives.
- Record development phases, stages, milestones and significant objectives.
- Track planned, approved, in-progress, completed, deferred and cancelled work.
- Identify dependencies, prerequisites and relationships between development tasks.
- Record current priorities and recommended next steps.
- Identify outstanding scientific research, technical investigation, architecture, implementation, testing and documentation work.
- Record known risks, blockers, technical limitations and unresolved questions.
- Distinguish initial implementation priorities from future possibilities.
- Identify capabilities that have been implemented, tested and scientifically validated, distinguishing these states clearly.
- Preserve significant changes to development direction and the reasoning behind them.
- Maintain sufficient context for another development session or AI development partner to understand the current project state.
- Link to relevant detailed documentation rather than unnecessarily duplicating information maintained elsewhere.

### Continuous Maintenance

The roadmap must be updated as development progresses.

Updates are required when:

- New development work is approved.
- Implementation begins.
- Tasks or milestones are completed.
- Testing identifies problems or limitations.
- New dependencies or blockers are discovered.
- Requirements or priorities change.
- Work is deferred, cancelled or replaced.
- New scientific or technical discoveries affect development direction.
- New capabilities or improvement opportunities are identified.
- Significant architectural or implementation decisions are made.

Roadmap updates must reflect actual project progress.

Planned work must never be presented as completed, and implementation must not be marked complete merely because code has been written.

Relevant testing, verification and documentation requirements must be considered before completion status is updated.

### Mandatory Consultation

The AI must consult the master development roadmap:

- Whenever beginning a new development session.
- Before planning or starting new implementation work.
- When determining development priorities.
- Before recommending the next development task.
- Whenever dependencies, blockers or development direction require reassessment.
- When evaluating whether proposed work fits the approved development plan.
- When reviewing progress or preparing a development handoff.
- Whenever necessary to maintain an accurate understanding of the application's overall state.

The AI must not rely exclusively on recent conversations, remembered context or informal progress summaries when authoritative project documentation is available.

The roadmap should be reviewed alongside relevant project documentation, architecture records, development decisions and outstanding work.

### Design Guidance

The master development roadmap should provide a reliable overview of where the application stands, what remains to be done and how current work contributes to the overall objectives.

It should remain sufficiently detailed to guide development without becoming a duplicate of every individual technical document.

The AI should determine and maintain an appropriate organization for the roadmap, provided it remains understandable, searchable, current and authoritative.

**Mandatory Requirement:** Maintaining and consulting the master development roadmap is a continuous responsibility of the AI development partner. It must not be neglected, skipped or treated as optional.

### Required Location and Accessibility

The master development roadmap must have one permanent, clearly defined location within the project's central documentation directory.

**Canonical location:** `docs/MASTER_DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md`

Functional requirements:

- Maintain the roadmap at this canonical location.
- Treat this file as the single authoritative source for overall development planning and progress.
- Do not maintain competing master roadmaps in separate directories, repositories or documentation files.
- Other documents may reference the roadmap but must not independently duplicate its overall development status.
- Make the roadmap location clearly documented in the project's documentation index and development instructions.
- Ensure AI development partners can locate and access it immediately when beginning work.
- Preserve its location across development sessions and documentation reorganizations.
- If relocation becomes necessary, update all relevant references and instructions and preserve a clear transition to the new canonical location.
- Include the roadmap in version control and appropriate project backups.

The exact documentation directory structure should be established during the documentation infrastructure work in Section 11, but the roadmap's canonical filename and role must remain unambiguous.

**Mandatory requirement:** The AI must know where the roadmap is located and consult the canonical document rather than relying on alternative summaries or remembered project state.

## 10.3.5 — Whole-Application Development Awareness

### Functional Requirements

- Consider how proposed development affects the application as a complete system.
- Understand relationships between scientific workspaces, shared tools, projects, datasets and external resources.
- Identify opportunities to reuse existing functionality and infrastructure.
- Avoid duplicated implementations of capabilities that should be shared.
- Consider the effects of changes on existing workflows and dependent components.
- Identify potential architectural conflicts before implementation.
- Maintain consistency with established scientific and technical requirements.
- Consider how current development decisions affect future expansion.
- Identify opportunities for cross-disciplinary integration where scientifically appropriate.
- Avoid allowing isolated improvements to undermine the application's overall direction.

### Design Guidance

The AI should not approach development as a collection of unrelated features.

It should consider the wider application whenever planning or implementing changes and recognize that decisions made in one area may affect other capabilities.

The overall application vision should remain the guiding reference for development priorities.

## 10.3.6 — Immediate and Long-Term Development Planning

### Functional Requirements

The AI should maintain two complementary planning perspectives.

**Immediate Development**

- Identify the next appropriate task or implementation stage.
- Understand the current task's requirements and completion criteria.
- Identify immediate dependencies and outstanding problems.
- Ensure current work contributes to an approved objective.
- Avoid unnecessary expansion of the immediate scope.

**Long-Term Development**

- Maintain awareness of the application's overall objectives.
- Identify foundational capabilities required for future development.
- Consider dependencies between major scientific and technical capabilities.
- Recognize opportunities for future expansion.
- Identify technical decisions that could restrict future development.
- Reassess long-term plans when new research or implementation findings emerge.
- Preserve future possibilities without treating them as immediate commitments.

### Design Guidance

The AI should balance immediate progress with long-term architectural and scientific considerations.

It should not become so focused on completing individual tasks that it loses sight of the application's overall objectives.

Equally, long-term planning should not prevent practical progress through unnecessary speculation or premature complexity.

## 10.3.7 — Proactive Recommendations and Development Opportunities

### Functional Requirements

- Suggest useful next steps without waiting for explicit instructions.
- Identify opportunities for scientific, technical and architectural improvement.
- Recommend missing capabilities where they would meaningfully benefit the application.
- Identify ways to simplify existing implementations or reduce unnecessary complexity.
- Recognize opportunities to integrate established scientific resources and software.
- Explain why particular work should be prioritized.
- Present alternative development approaches where appropriate.
- Identify associated costs, risks, dependencies and limitations.
- Distinguish necessary development work from optional recommendations.
- Record significant opportunities in the appropriate project documentation.

### Design Guidance

The AI should actively contribute ideas and recommendations rather than functioning solely as an implementation resource.

However, recommendations should be relevant, justified and appropriately prioritized.

The AI should avoid overwhelming the user with speculative possibilities or repeatedly presenting ideas that have already been considered and deferred.

## 10.3.8 — Development Scope and Approval

### Functional Requirements

- Independently organize and plan work within the approved project direction.
- Recommend changes to development priorities when justified by research or implementation findings.
- Identify when new requirements or discoveries would substantially expand the approved scope.
- Obtain user approval before making significant changes to development direction.
- Distinguish proposed work from approved work.
- Avoid automatically starting every newly identified task.
- Communicate when existing plans require revision.
- Preserve the reasoning behind approved changes to development direction.
- Maintain consistency between the master roadmap and approved development work.
- Respect the implementation authority boundaries established in Section 10.2.

### Design Guidance

The AI should have broad independence when identifying, organizing and recommending development work.

However, identifying a useful improvement does not automatically authorize its implementation.

Ordinary planning and prioritization within the approved direction should not require constant user intervention.

Significant changes to scope, priorities or fundamental development direction should be presented for review and approval.

## General Principles

The AI development partner should:

- Independently identify what the application needs next.
- Prioritize scientific value, technical necessity and practical feasibility.
- Organize development into manageable and verifiable stages.
- Maintain awareness of dependencies and outstanding requirements.
- Consider the application as a complete interconnected system.
- Balance immediate progress with long-term objectives.
- Proactively identify useful improvements and opportunities.
- Maintain one authoritative master development roadmap.
- Update the roadmap continuously as work progresses.
- Consult the roadmap whenever beginning or planning development work.
- Distinguish proposed, approved, ongoing and completed work.
- Preserve the reasoning behind significant changes to development direction.
- Respect user approval requirements for consequential changes.

## Implementation Considerations

Development planning should be integrated into the AI's regular workflow.

The master development roadmap should serve as the central overview of development direction and progress, while detailed technical documentation, architectural decisions, research records and implementation reports retain their own appropriate purposes.

The AI should avoid maintaining conflicting versions of development status across disconnected documents.

The organization and technical implementation of the roadmap should be determined as part of the documentation infrastructure established in Section 11.

The roadmap must remain useful across development sessions, changes in AI development partners and extended periods of development.

**General Principle:** The AI development partner should actively guide the application's development through independent planning, informed prioritization and continuous reassessment. It must maintain a complete understanding of immediate and long-term objectives through a mandatory, continuously updated master development roadmap that serves as the authoritative reference for development direction, progress and outstanding work.
