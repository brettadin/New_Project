# 12.1 — Initial Research Objectives and Scope

## Purpose

Establish the objectives, scope and expectations of the initial research phase before application implementation begins.

The AI development partner must develop a sufficiently comprehensive understanding of the application's scientific purpose, intended capabilities, technical requirements and practical constraints before recommending an implementation strategy.

The initial research phase should establish a reliable foundation for subsequent technical planning and development.

## 12.1.1 Initial Research Objectives

**Core requirements**

- Develop a comprehensive understanding of the application's overall vision, purpose and intended scientific capabilities.
- Review the complete requirements documentation and identify relationships between sections.
- Investigate the scientific disciplines and research activities the application intends to support.
- Understand the scientific principles, methodologies, mathematical foundations and computational requirements underlying its intended capabilities.
- Investigate existing scientific software, databases, libraries, frameworks and external resources.
- Identify technical requirements, dependencies, constraints and potential implementation difficulties.
- Evaluate opportunities to reuse established scientific tools and infrastructure.
- Identify missing information, unresolved questions and potentially conflicting requirements.
- Assess the practicality of the proposed application scope.
- Produce research findings and recommendations that support informed development planning.

## 12.1.2 Review of Existing Requirements

**Core requirements**

- Review all approved application requirements before beginning substantial research.
- Understand the relationships between scientific capabilities, workspaces, projects, shared tools and external resources.
- Identify requirements that depend on other capabilities or infrastructure.
- Identify areas requiring additional scientific or technical investigation.
- Identify requirements that may be impractical, unnecessarily complicated, duplicative or insufficiently defined.
- Preserve the original intent of requirements when evaluating alternative approaches.
- Record significant findings, uncertainties and recommendations.
- Avoid silently changing approved requirements during the research phase.

**Design guidance**

The AI should evaluate the requirements as one interconnected application rather than investigating each section in isolation.

Research should consider how individual capabilities contribute to the overall system and how their implementation may affect other areas.

## 12.1.3 Research Scope and Depth

**Core requirements**

Initial research should investigate the subjects necessary to establish a scientifically credible and technically practical development foundation.

Research should include, where relevant:

- Scientific principles and established methodologies.
- Mathematical, physical and chemical foundations.
- Observational techniques and instrumentation.
- Scientific datasets, archives and reference resources.
- Data formats, structures, metadata and interoperability.
- Existing scientific software and computational libraries.
- Analytical methods, models and simulations.
- Visualization and scientific data presentation.
- Technical architecture and implementation requirements.
- Dependencies, compatibility and integration requirements.
- Performance, computational resources and storage.
- Security, privacy, licensing and long-term maintenance.
- Relevant limitations, uncertainties and unresolved problems.

Research depth should reflect the complexity and scientific importance of each capability.

Not every future possibility requires comprehensive investigation before initial development. Prioritize research according to the application's immediate foundations, dependencies and planned development stages.

## 12.1.4 Independent Research and Investigation

**Core requirements**

- The AI development partner must independently identify and investigate relevant scientific and technical subjects.
- Research should not be restricted to resources or technologies already mentioned in the requirements.
- Identify established approaches, alternative methodologies and potentially useful capabilities.
- Investigate relevant developments in scientific research and software ecosystems.
- Evaluate the credibility, reliability and applicability of information.
- Prefer authoritative scientific publications, official technical documentation and established resources.
- Distinguish established capabilities from experimental, emerging or unverified approaches.
- Preserve references, findings, limitations and research dates.
- Identify unsuccessful investigations and explain their outcomes where relevant.
- Make recommendations based on evidence and practical suitability rather than familiarity or convenience.

Routine public research does not require individual user approval. Restrictions on external access, private information, financial commitments and consequential actions remain governed by Section 10.2.

## 12.1.5 Identification of Knowledge Gaps and Risks

**Core requirements**

- Identify scientific concepts or methodologies that require further understanding.
- Identify technical uncertainties and implementation constraints.
- Identify missing requirements or insufficiently defined functionality.
- Identify dependencies that may affect development sequencing.
- Identify potential conflicts between scientific objectives and technical feasibility.
- Assess significant security, privacy, licensing, maintenance and resource risks.
- Distinguish confirmed problems from potential risks and unresolved questions.
- Recommend additional investigation when necessary.
- Avoid proceeding on unsupported assumptions when they could materially affect scientific accuracy or architecture.

**Design guidance**

Not every uncertainty needs to be resolved immediately. Some questions can remain open until the relevant development stage, provided their implications and dependencies are understood.

## 12.1.6 Research Documentation and Deliverables

**Core requirements**

The initial research phase must produce organized and accessible documentation of its significant findings.

Research deliverables should include:

- Scientific research findings and supporting references.
- Technical research and technology evaluations.
- Identified scientific and technical requirements.
- Relevant external resources and integration opportunities.
- Dependencies and compatibility considerations.
- Identified risks, limitations and unresolved questions.
- Recommendations and alternative approaches.
- Initial development priorities and prerequisites.
- Information required for architectural and implementation planning.

Significant research must be recorded using the documentation procedures and templates established in Section 11.

Research findings must remain distinguishable from recommendations and approved decisions.

## 12.1.7 Research Completion and Readiness

**Core requirements**

The initial research phase should be considered sufficiently complete when:

- The application's scientific objectives and major capabilities are adequately understood.
- The primary scientific methods and requirements have been investigated.
- Relevant scientific resources and software options have been evaluated.
- Major technical requirements and constraints have been identified.
- Significant dependencies and risks are understood.
- Important knowledge gaps and unresolved questions have been documented.
- A practical initial development scope can be recommended.
- Sufficient information exists to begin detailed technical and architectural planning.

Completion does not require every future capability or unresolved scientific question to have been fully investigated.

Research should continue throughout development as new requirements, technologies, methods and opportunities arise.

## General Principles

- Research must precede implementation where scientific or technical understanding is necessary.
- The AI development partner must investigate independently and critically.
- Research should cover the complete application while prioritizing foundational requirements.
- Scientific credibility and technical practicality must guide recommendations.
- Knowledge gaps, assumptions and risks must remain visible.
- Significant findings must be documented and traceable.
- Research completion should be based on readiness for the next development stage, not an arbitrary requirement to investigate everything.

**General principle:** The initial research phase must establish a sufficiently strong scientific and technical understanding of the application to support informed architectural decisions and practical development planning without committing prematurely to implementation choices.
