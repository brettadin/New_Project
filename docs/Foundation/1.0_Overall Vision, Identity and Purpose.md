# 1. Overall Vision, Identity and Purpose

This section establishes the application's identity, central purpose, overall vision and guiding philosophy. It defines the kind of scientific platform the application is intended to become, the problems it aims to address, the users it should serve and the principles that should guide its development.

The application is envisioned primarily as a comprehensive astronomy research platform, bringing together scientific tools, data discovery, analysis, modelling and visualization within a unified research environment. Education, project management, collaboration and community functionality should complement and strengthen this central purpose.

The section also establishes the application's independence, accessibility, scientific credibility and long-term ambition, providing the foundation for the more detailed scientific, functional and technical requirements that follow.

### 1.1 — Project Identity and Independence

Establishes the application as an entirely new project developed independently from existing applications. Defines its separate identity, name, branding, architecture, codebase, documentation and development history. The application's name and branding remain undecided.

### 1.2 — Overall Vision and Purpose

Defines the ambition to create a comprehensive astronomy research platform that brings together scientific investigation, observational data, analytical tools, modelling, visualization and access to external scientific resources. Establishes scientific research as the application's primary purpose.

### 1.3 — Integrated Scientific Research Environment

Defines the application as more than a collection of unrelated tools or an interface for launching external software. Establishes the relationship between scientific capabilities, research workspaces, projects, education and community functionality, emphasizing coherent integration wherever scientifically and technically appropriate.

### 1.4 — Intended Audience and Accessibility

Establishes the broad range of intended users and the expectation that the application should accommodate different levels of scientific and technical experience. Beginner accessibility must not restrict advanced functionality, while professional scientific capabilities should remain approachable and understandable.

### 1.5 — Core Philosophy and Guiding Principles

Defines the principles that should guide the entire application, including scientific integrity, reliable methodologies, transparency, reproducibility, usability, modularity, extensibility and user control. Establishes that functionality should contribute to a coherent scientific environment rather than simply maximizing feature count.

### 1.6 — Long-Term Ambition and Development Direction

Preserves the application's ability to expand into additional astronomical disciplines, related sciences and advanced research capabilities. Establishes incremental development guided by scientific value, technical feasibility, practical usefulness and appropriate prioritization, without treating the initial scope as a permanent limitation.

## Overall Principles

- The application is an independent project with its own identity and development history.
- Scientific astronomy research is its primary purpose.
- Scientific tools and capabilities should operate within a coherent, integrated research environment.
- Education, project management, collaboration and community functionality should complement the central scientific purpose.
- The application should accommodate beginners and experienced researchers without sacrificing scientific depth.
- Scientific credibility, transparency, reproducibility and user control are fundamental.
- Functionality should be integrated meaningfully rather than accumulated without a coherent purpose.
- The application should remain modular, extensible and adaptable.
- Initial development should establish a practical foundation for the broader long-term vision.
- Future scientific and technical expansion should remain open to consideration.

**General principle:** The application should become a comprehensive, scientifically credible and accessible astronomy research environment that brings together the tools, data, knowledge and workflows needed to investigate astronomical subjects. Its development should remain guided by scientific value, practical usefulness and a coherent long-term vision.

## Implementation Boundary

Section 1 establishes the application's identity, purpose, overall vision and guiding principles. It does not prescribe specific scientific capabilities, technical architecture, development technologies, interface layouts or implementation details.

These are defined in the subsequent sections and must be evaluated through the research, planning and approval processes established throughout the Foundations documentation.

The application's name, branding and other identity-specific design decisions remain open for later consideration.
